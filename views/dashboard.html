    How to read this

    What is counted and what is still missing.

    • Company totals count a two man job once. Each tech's own numbers count it for both.
    • Weeks run Monday to Sunday and are cut at month end, so Sep W5 and Oct W1 are short.
    • Revenue is paid invoices. Platinum plan sales (paid about $1,500 or more) are not jobs and not revenue.
    • Job classes follow the charge rules, not the tags. Before October they are not shown.
    • Derek's numbers are computed from Housecall Pro and should be checked once against the KPI sheet. Clayton's September rows are the verified ones.
    • Labor and cost of goods are not shown here. Labor will get its own private tab. Cost of goods starts 10/19 once unit costs are filled in.
    • Marcone is read from email. A customer shows as Unmatched when the job could not be found, and STOCK means van or shop stock.
